# WNP MHW — 120–155E, 15–35N Summary (2025-10-14)

## Objective
- Analyze interannual variability of annual MHW days over 120–155E, 15–35N, decompose into short/long components (5‑yr cut), and relate to ENSO (NINO3.4).
- Standardize series by std only (no mean removal) for dimensionless comparability. Use area weighting (cos(lat)).

## Data & Domain
- MHW days (monthly, OISST): `/Volumes/HJPARK4/MHW/source/detect_hobday/mhw_days_wnp_1982_2022_bl1987_2017.nc`
- ENSO index: `/Volumes/HJPARK4/MHW/data/indices/nino3.4.d` (monthly ASCII; 5‑month running mean).
- Region: 120–155E, 15–35N (area‑weighted by cos(lat)).
- Years: 1982‑01 to 2022‑12 (monthly axis), annual series plotted at mid‑year (~July 15).

## Processing (final choices)
- Area‑weighted annual series from monthly MHW days.
- Std‑normalize by dividing by std (no demeaning).
- 5‑yr decomposition on annual series: LP = 5‑yr cutoff low‑pass (interior: zero‑phase; boundary: trailing 5‑yr at series end), HP = orig/std − LP.
- ENSO shading: NINO3.4 5‑mo RM; positive=orange, negative=blue.
- ELT shading (gray): 1983, 1988, 1995, 1998, 2010, 2016, 2020.
- Dynamic NT shading (light‑green): years with HP>0 and not ELT (boundary policy: 2021 evaluated with trailing 5‑yr LP).

## Decisions
- Use 5‑yr cut (HP<5 yr, LP≥5 yr) on area‑weighted annual series for main figures.
- Std‑normalize as default for this type of overlay unless otherwise specified.
- ELT set: canonical + 1995 only (no 2005/2007). NT: dynamic (HP>0 & non‑ELT; boundary trailing 5‑yr for 2021) = 1984, 1991, 1994, 1999, 2001, 2004, 2008, 2013, 2017, 2021.
