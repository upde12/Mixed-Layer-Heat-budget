# de Boyer Montégut et al. (2004) — JGR, MLD Definition/Climatology

- Citation: Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans (2004), 109(C12003). DOI:10.1029/2004JC002378
- Local PDF: `../deBoyerMontegut_etal_2004_JGR_MLD.pdf`

Focus
- Global MLD climatology and methodology; density- and temperature‑based criteria.

Relation to MLHB
- Not a direct MLHB analysis. Provides the canonical Δσ₀ threshold method (e.g., 0.03 kg m⁻³ at 10 m reference) used to define h in MLHB.

Key points for our pipeline
- Recommends density threshold with shallow reference depth to avoid skin effects.
- Reports sensitivity to threshold choice (e.g., 0.02–0.05) and regional/seasonal variations.
- Underpins the default `--mld-source recompute` with Δσ₀=0.03, ref=10 m.
