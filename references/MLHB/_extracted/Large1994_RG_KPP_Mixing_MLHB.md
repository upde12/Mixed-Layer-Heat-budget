# Large, McWilliams & Doney (1994) — Reviews of Geophysics, KPP

- Citation: Reviews of Geophysics (1994), 32(4), 363–403. DOI:10.1029/94RG01872
- Local PDF: `../Large_McWilliams_Doney_1994_KPP_review.pdf`

Focus
- Nonlocal K‑Profile Parameterization (KPP) for ocean boundary layer mixing.

How ML heat budget is analyzed
- Not a direct MLHB study; provides the vertical mixing closure widely used to estimate vertical diffusive heat flux at the base of the mixed layer (DIFFV) and informs entrainment representations.

Key results (relevant to MLHB)
- Vertical diffusivity profiles and nonlocal transport terms prescribe realistic entrainment/detrainment impacts on T profiles.
- Highlights dependence of boundary layer depth and entrainment on wind stress, buoyancy forcing, and shear—inputs that modulate ENT and DIFFV terms in MLHB.

Notes
- Use as conceptual/parameterization reference when interpreting DIFFV or when comparing bulk entrainment against KPP-based tendencies.
