# Monterey & Levitus (1997) — NOAA NESDIS Atlas 14

- Title: Seasonal variability of mixed layer depth for the world ocean
- Repository file: `../Monterey_Levitus_1997_NOAA_Atlas14.pdf`

Focus
- Global atlas of MLD seasonal variability using historical hydrographic profiles.

How MLD is defined/derived
- Presents temperature- and density-based criteria; documents regional/seasonal contrasts and uncertainties tied to criterion choice.
- Offers climatological maps and seasonal cycles of MLD that serve as a baseline for model/data comparisons.

Key points (relevant to MLHB/operations)
- Density-based thresholds are generally more robust across salinity‑affected regions; temperature-only criteria can be biased in strong haline stratification.
- Strong seasonal cycles: shallow summer MLD (few–tens of meters) vs deep winter MLD (order 50–200+ m) vary by basin.
- Provides canonical ranges useful for sanity checks and mask design (e.g., shallow shelf vs open ocean).

Notes
- Atlas predates TEOS‑10; when comparing to modern recomputation, account for equation‑of‑state and gridding differences.
