# Price, Weller & Pinkel (1986) — JGR, Diurnal Cycling

- Citation: Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans (1986), 91(C7), 8411–8427.
- Local PDF: `../Price_Weller_Pinkel_1986_JGR_DiurnalCycling.pdf`

Focus
- Upper-ocean response to diurnal heating/cooling and wind mixing using observations and a bulk model (PWP).

How ML heat budget is analyzed
- Mixed-layer temperature tendency decomposed into surface heat flux forcing and entrainment/mixing at the base of the mixed layer.
- Entrainment diagnosed via bulk Richardson criterion; effectively active during deepening phases (nighttime cooling/wind mixing).
- Vertical diffusion within and below the mixed layer is represented with bulk mixing plus a shear-dependent parameterization.

Key results (relevant to MLHB)
- Nighttime entrainment generates cooling of the mixed layer that offsets daytime surface heating, controlling net diurnal SST amplitude.
- Entrainment magnitude scales with wind stress and preexisting stratification; deepening-only perspective aligns with observed cooling episodes.
- Provides a foundational framework for separating surface flux vs. entrainment contributions in diurnal-to-event scales.

Notes
- Paper is not framed as a climatological MLHB, but methodology underpins entrainment term design and interpretation in event-scale budgets.
